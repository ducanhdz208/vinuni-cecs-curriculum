Discrepancies Found in the Source Documents

1. Algorithm Design course code. The 2025–2026 sample study plan (Year 2 – Semester 2) lists Algorithm Design as COMP3020, but COMP3020 is Machine Learning everywhere else, including the learning roadmap diagram in the same document. This explorer uses COMP3010 = Algorithm Design and COMP3020 = Machine Learning, matching the 2024–2028 Curriculum Framework.

2. Intro to Engineering and CS code. The block diagram keeps CECS1010 at 2 credits; the sample study plan uses CECS1011 at 2 credits. This explorer uses CECS1011.

3. HASS1010 / HASS1020 naming. Both documents give English titles that are swapped relative to their own Vietnamese titles (HASS1010 is "Triết học Mác-Lênin" = Marxist-Leninist Philosophy, but is titled "Political Economy" in English). The official English titles are used here verbatim.

4. Year 2 Semester 1 credit total. The listed courses sum to 14 graded credits + Community Service Learning (non-credit); the document states 16. The 2-credit gap is likely the Sustainability & Global Citizenship course group being double-listed. Totals here follow the published figures.

Program Profile

Name of the program degree: Bachelor of Science in Computer Science

Program code: 7480101 · VQF level: Bachelor's degree – level 6

Applicable to: students entering AY 2025–2026 (Cohort 6)

Administrative college: College of Engineering and Computer Science

Program duration: 4 years, full-time, face-to-face (campus-based), starting September

Language of delivery: English

Program Director: Prof. Dr. Wray Buntine

Total credits:

  • Option 1 — Single major: 120 credits
  • Option 2 — Major + Minor in Robotics, Product Design, Technopreneur or another minor: 135 credits

International accreditation: FIBAA (2025), QS 5 star (2024). Developed with and validated by Cornell University (USA); benchmarked domestically against Hanoi University of Science and Technology.

The purpose of the program is to develop computer scientists with a deep understanding of underlying computer science theoretical principles, software engineering practices and significant exposure to problem-solving, project-based, authentic and hands-on learning experience which will prepare them for work and allow them to contribute to society as creative, innovative and personally well rounded.

Program Educational Objectives

Within a few years of graduation, the majority of graduates will demonstrate excellence in (i) top graduate programs, (ii) technical and managerial leadership tracks in technology-based industries, or (iii) entrepreneurial ventures. In these roles they will:

  1. Apply basic knowledge of computer science principles and in-depth knowledge of specific computing topics (AI, ML, software engineering practice) to address technical and societal problems.
  2. Conceive, design, and realize useful products, systems, and services, respecting economic, environmental, cultural, life safety, and ethical constraints.
  3. Discover and apply new knowledge, and develop new tools for the practice of computer science or software development.
  4. Be sensitive to professional and societal contexts, committed to ethical action, and engaged in life-long learning.
  5. Be leaders with an entrepreneurial mindset, and effective communicators in multidisciplinary teams.
  6. Engage with their communities, profession, the nation and the world.

Assessment & Graduation

Admission assessment: AQI, NAQI, and interviews, scored on Ability, Aspiration, Commitment and Creativity.

Progress assessment: MCQ and written exams, presentations, major assignments, projects, essays and research reports.

Exit assessment: final-year dissertation (Capstone Design, 6 credits across two semesters).

Graduation requirement: achieve the required number of accumulated credits (minimum 120 earned credits; students may take up to 135 within the allowed timeframe without additional tuition).

Teaching paradigm: Active Learning — lectures, discussions, group presentations, practical exercises, course projects, graduation project, internship and field trips, research projects.
