Bachelor of Science in Computer Science

Curriculum Framework · Cohort 2024-2028

Program Profile

Name of the program degree: Bachelor of Science in Computer Science

Program duration: to be completed in 4 years on a full-time basis

Total credits:

  • Option 1 Single major: 120 credits
  • Option 2 Major + Minor in Robotics or Product Design or Technopreneur or another minor: 135 credits

The purpose of the program is to develop computer scientists with a deep understanding of underlying computer science theoretical principles, software engineering practices and significant exposure to problem-solving, project-based, authentic and hands-on learning experience which will prepare them for work and allow them to contribute to society as creative, innovative and personally well rounded.

Program Educational Objectives

  1. Apply basic knowledge of computer science principles and in-depth knowledge of specific computing topics (AI, ML, software engineering) to address technical and societal problems.
  2. Conceive, design, and realize useful products, systems, and services, respecting economic, environmental, cultural, life safety, and ethical constraints.
  3. Discover and apply new knowledge, and develop new tools for the practice of computer science.
  4. Be sensitive to professional and societal contexts, committed to ethical action, and engaged in life-long learning.
  5. Be leaders with an entrepreneurial mindset, and effective communicators in multidisciplinary teams.
  6. Engage with their communities, profession, the nation and the world.

Curriculum Composition (120 Credits)

  • I. VINCORE (31 Credits | 25.8%)
    • Enterprise & Innovation (4 cr), Leadership Mindset (2 cr), Civic Responsibility (2 cr), Ethics (2 cr)
    • Community Service (45 hrs), Working with Brain (2 cr), Working with Others (4 cr), Working with Self (90 hrs)
    • Integrated Vietnam Studies (11 cr), Sustainability & Global Citizenship (2 cr), Creative Arts (2 cr)

  • II. PROFESSIONAL EDUCATION (89 Credits | 74.2%)
    • College Core Requirement (28 cr)
    • Major (Discipline) Requirement (32 cr)
    • Area of Concentration Elective (12 cr)
    • Free Electives (7 cr)
    • Science Courses (2 cr), Course-related Projects (2 cr)
    • Practice/Internship (Non-credit), Graduation Thesis/Capstone (6 cr)
